# Pear bread

 - 2 cups flour
 - 1/2 tsp baking soda
 - 2 tsp baking powder
 - pinch or two of salt
 - 1/2 cup sugar (I mix white and brown)
 - 3/4 oat milk
 - 1/3 cup oil
 - 1/2 tsp of vanilla, or more to taste
 - 1 large egg
 - 1 pear, cubed
 - 1/2 cup diced pecans
 - 2 to 3 tbsp ginger

Preheat oven to 350F. Mix the dry ingredients, then the wet. I mix a bit of brown sugar and extra diced pecans and sprinkle onto the top.

Cooking time depends on the pan, 45 minutes?

Delicious!
